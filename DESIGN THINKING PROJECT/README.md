# 🚀 Design Thinking Project: CyberCare (CC) - A Smart Online Counseling Platform for Cyberbullying Victims

**Project Duration:** 21 October 2025 – 10 January 2026  
**Presentation Date:** 14 January 2026  
**Course:** Technology and Information System (TIS – SECP 1513) Semester 1  

---

Cyberbullying is a serious issue that can lead to anxiety, low self-esteem, and depression, especially among teenagers and young adults. Many victims hesitate to seek help due to privacy concerns, fear of judgment, and limited access to counselors.  

**To address these challenges, CyberCare offers:**

- 🤖 **AI-powered chat** for immediate emotional support  
- 🧑‍⚕️ **Real counseling sessions** with trained professionals (online or face-to-face)  
- 📍 **Counselor selection** based on qualifications and location  
- 📅 **Flexible booking system** for convenient session scheduling  

---

The journey of building CyberCare was not easy. It involved months of trial and error, technical issues, revisions, and continuous improvements. Many problems had to be fixed repeatedly, but we stayed committed and pushed through until the platform was fully completed 💻🔥. This experience taught me the importance of **consistency, resilience, and perseverance**, especially when facing challenges.

Throughout the project, we applied the full **Design Thinking process** — **empathizing** with users, **defining** privacy as the key issue, **ideating** practical solutions, **prototyping** the website, and **testing** it to improve usability and effectiveness. Each phase strengthened my problem-solving skills and deepened my understanding of how real-world digital solutions are developed.
