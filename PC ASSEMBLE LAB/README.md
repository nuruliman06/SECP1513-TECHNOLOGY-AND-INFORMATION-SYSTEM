 # 🖥️ PC Assemble & Maintenance Lab 

**Date:** 5 November 2025  
**Time:** 12:00 – 1:00 p.m.  
**Venue:** N28, Level 3, Software Innovation Lab  

---

## 📝 Summarization
The PC Assemble and Maintenance Lab was my first hands-on experience with computer hardware. During the session, the class was divided into groups and guided by the instructor and lab assistants.

We were instructed step by step to disassemble a desktop computer, beginning with opening the casing and disconnecting the cables, followed by removing components such as the power supply, RAM, hard disk, and cooling fan. Afterward, we reassembled all components and tested the system. Successfully powering on the PC indicated that the assembly process was carried out correctly.

At the end of the lab session, a short quiz was conducted based on the PC maintenance activities performed.

---

## 💡 Personal Reflection
This lab enhanced my understanding of internal computer components and their functions. As it was my first experience assembling and disassembling a PC, it helped build my confidence in handling hardware components and following technical instructions. The session also emphasized the importance of proper handling, teamwork, and attention to detail in technical tasks. Overall, the lab was a valuable learning experience that strengthened my practical knowledge of PC maintenance.
