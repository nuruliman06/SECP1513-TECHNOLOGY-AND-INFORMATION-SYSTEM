---
## Course Synopsis
This course introduces fundamental concepts and applications of technology and information systems, covering hardware, software, networks, data management, and cybersecurity. Students learn through lectures, discussions, and hands-on projects, developing skills in problem-solving, teamwork, and critical thinking. By the end, students can analyze information systems, apply concepts to real-world contexts, and design simple technology solutions.
