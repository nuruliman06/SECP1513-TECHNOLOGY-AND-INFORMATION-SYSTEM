## 📝Summarization
From the industrial visit, I learned the main reasons why projects fail, including:  
- **Poor preparation** – unclear planning can cause delays and confusion in projects.  
- **Weak leadership** – affects team performance and decision-making.  
- **Ignoring warning signs** – small issues can escalate into bigger problems.  
- **Poor communication** – leads to misunderstandings and mistakes.  

During the visit, I explored several technology booths, which gave me hands-on exposure to current IT solutions and industry tools:  
- **Dell Technologies** – learned about different laptop types and their uses for students and professionals.  
- **Crossroad & VST ECS Pericomp** – got exposure to IT products and solutions supporting digital transformation.  
- **D-Ron** – understood the importance of system and data security.  
- **Microsoft Copilot** – saw how it differs from ChatGPT, offers better protection, and is widely used by UTM students.

---

## 🛠️ Suggested Improvements
- The visit could be enhanced with more hands-on demonstrations at the booths.  
- Longer Q&A sessions would allow participants to interact more and understand the technologies in depth.

---

## ⭐ Personal Reflection
This industrial visit increased my interest in **project management, system development, and cybersecurity-related technologies**. I realized that **technical skills alone are not enough**; soft skills such as **communication, leadership, and teamwork** are equally important in real-world IT projects.
