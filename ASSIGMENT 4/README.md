# 🚢 Industrial Visit 2: Johor Port Berhad – Information & Communication Technology Department

**📅 Date:** 8 January 2026  
**⏰ Time:** 12:00 P.M. - 5:00 P.M.  
**📍 Venue:** Johor Port Berhad, Pasir Gudang, Johor  

I recently had the opportunity to attend Industrial Talk 2 at Johor Port Berhad, delivered by En. Mohamad Taufiq Abdul Razak (IT Manager) and Tn. Hj. Badrul Hisham Abdul Halim (Head of Infrastructure). It was inspiring to see how technology keeps one of Malaysia’s busiest ports running smoothly.

---

## 📝 Summary
During the session, I learned how the port’s ICT teams manage **critical infrastructure, including broadband networks, 4G LTE**, and servers at the **Data Center and Disaster Recovery Center** to ensure operations are always reliable.  

The **Business Applications** team focuses on the **JPBI** portal, the main platform for container, bulk, and marine operations. Using **low-code developmen**t, they quickly create and improve internal workflows, automating manual tasks to make processes faster and more accurate. Meanwhile, **Python** and traditional programming are used for **core backend systems** to ensure **stability and scalability**.  

Other teams handle **project governance**, cybersecurity, and technical support, ensuring all systems comply with **ISO/IEC** standards, sensitive data is protected, and staff can resolve IT issues efficiently. The port also uses **data analysis, MetroCluster solutions, and disaster recovery systems** to maintain continuous operations even during unexpected events like power outages or cyberattacks.
