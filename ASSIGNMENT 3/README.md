## 📝 Summary

I had the opportunity to attend **Industry Talk 2** conducted by **Serunai Commerce Sdn Bhd**, presented by **Ts. Hj. Abdul Alim bin Abdul Muttalib**. The session provided valuable exposure to how **project management** and **system development** are applied in real workplace environments and why they are essential in the IT industry.

The speaker shared insights on project management, explaining that successful projects require proper planning, coordination, and teamwork to ensure smooth execution. He also discussed the differences between:

- **Waterfall methodology:** structured and sequential flow  
- **Agile methodology:** emphasizes flexibility, iteration, and continuous feedback during development  

A major focus of the talk was the **System Development Life Cycle (SDLC)**, which consists of 7 stages:

1. **Planning**  
2. **Requirements Gathering**  
3. **System Design**  
4. **Development**  
5. **Testing**  
6. **Deployment**  
7. **Maintenance** 

To make the concept easier to understand, he used a **cooking analogy**, highlighting that just like cooking requires preparation and following proper steps, system development also needs a structured process to achieve successful results.

The session also highlighted how project management and system development contribute to computer networks and security, particularly in managing risks and ensuring system reliability.

## 💡 Personal Reflection

From this talk, I realized that **learning without true understanding** and **without knowing how to apply knowledge to real-world problems** can limit future career opportunities. It reinforced that **project management** and **system development** are fundamental in computer science.  

By **improving my programming skills, applying SDLC principles, and strengthening teamwork abilities**, I am preparing for a successful career in **cybersecurity**.
